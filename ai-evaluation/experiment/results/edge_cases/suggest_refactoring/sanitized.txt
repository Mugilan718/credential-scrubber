**1. Refactoring:** Group the flat keys into namespaced prefixes (e.g. `server.*`, `db.*`, `app.*`) instead of leaving unrelated settings as bare top-level keys.

**2. Why it's an improvement:** Right now `timeout`, `port`, `environment`, `country`, `username`, `database`, and the benchmark-only fields (`ghost_writer`, `concert_hall`, `authored_by`, `tokenizer_version`) all sit at the same flat level with no indication of which subsystem they belong to. Namespacing groups related settings, makes ownership/scope obvious at a glance, avoids future key collisions (e.g. a second `username` for a different service), and lets tooling (or humans) filter/validate a subsystem's config as a unit.

**3. Before/After:**

Before:
```properties
timeout = 5000
port = 8080
environment = "production"
country = "India"
username = "admin"
database = "testdb"
```

After:
```properties
server.port = 8080
server.timeout = 5000

app.environment = "production"
app.country = "India"

db.name = "testdb"
db.username = "admin"
```
